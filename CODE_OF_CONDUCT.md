# Code of Conduct

This project adapts the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), version 2.1.

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in AgentProof a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, colour, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Behaviour that builds this community:

- Showing empathy and kindness toward others.
- Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback.
- Accepting responsibility for our mistakes, apologising to those affected, and learning from the experience.
- Focusing on what is best for the community, not only for ourselves.

Behaviour that is unacceptable:

- Sexualised language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address, without their explicit permission.
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions, and commit history — and when an individual is officially representing the project in public spaces.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour through the repository's private **Security → Report a vulnerability** flow, which is the confidential channel described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). It is the only private channel this project publishes, so conduct reports use it too. Do not open a public issue for a conduct report.

Include what happened, where, when, and links to the relevant issues, pull requests, or comments. Reports are reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly. Maintainers are obligated to respect the privacy and security of the reporter.

Maintainers should acknowledge a report within seven days. No response deadline beyond that is promised.

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards, and will take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any behaviour they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful. They have the right to remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that are not aligned with this Code of Conduct, and will communicate the reasons for a moderation decision when it is appropriate to do so.

Enforcement follows the Contributor Covenant's escalation ladder: a private written warning, a warning with a period of no interaction, a temporary ban, and finally a permanent ban. The applicable step depends on the severity and the pattern of the violation, not on who reported it.
